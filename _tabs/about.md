Welcome to my blog! I am writing about my explorations into the meaning of meaning and everything else.

I have a couple objectives for this website. This will be my main external blog on issues that I am thinking through. One of my goals is to create a set of topical pages that provide my opinions on otherwise controversial topics. I feel as though I have avoided doing the work to understand these topics for long enough and I actually want to have well-researched opinions on the matter. I want to collect quality arguments and papers that articulate my stance. Some topics that I plan to include are... Evolution, Climate Change, Consciousness, and the Historicity of the Gospels.
